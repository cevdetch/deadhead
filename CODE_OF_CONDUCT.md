# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Keep discussions focused on the work,
explain disagreements with evidence, and give people room to ask questions and
correct mistakes.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, and sharing another
person's private information without consent are unacceptable. These standards
apply to this project's issues, pull requests, and other project spaces.

## Reporting concerns

Cevdet ([cevdetch](https://github.com/cevdetch)) handles conduct reports.
For a concern that needs private handling, open an issue titled **Private conduct
report** asking for a private contact channel. Include no incident details,
names of affected people, or private information in that public request. Share
the details after the maintainer provides a private channel.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may ask participants to edit a comment, remove content, restrict
participation, or ban an account, depending on the severity and recurrence of the
behavior. Participants can ask the maintainer to reconsider an action through
the same reporting process.
